# Operations Intelligence Case Studies

A portfolio of real-world case studies focused on integrations, automation, technical operations, data quality, and operational intelligence.

These cases are based on business problems I worked on in a SaaS support and operations environment. All sensitive information has been anonymized, generalized, or recreated for demonstration purposes.

## About this portfolio

I work at the intersection of **operations, technology, and data**.

My focus is on designing practical systems that:

- Reduce manual work
- Improve operational visibility
- Increase data reliability
- Standardize support and integration workflows
- Connect technical execution with business impact

This repository is not intended to showcase only code. It is designed to show how I approach ambiguous operational problems, structure information, make technical decisions, and turn fragmented workflows into repeatable processes.

## Case Studies

| Case Study | Focus Area | What it demonstrates |

| [Intercom Data Cleanup & Salesforce Validation](./cases/intercom-data-cleanup-validation/case-study.md) | Data Quality / Cross-system Validation | Structured a validation workflow for 16,513 Intercom user records, increasing merchant match coverage and reducing ambiguous investigation cases. |
| [Integrations Copilot: AI + Slack + Automation](./cases/integrations-copilot-ai-automation/case-study.md) | AI Operations / Integration Triage | Built an AI-assisted Slack workflow to centralize integration research across native options, Zapier availability, and internal guidelines. |
| [Integrations Monitoring Dashboard](./cases/integrations-monitoring-dashboard/case-study.md) | BI / Data Modeling / Operational Monitoring | Designed a hybrid data model combining Zapier, Salesforce, and Mixpanel data to monitor integration health, quantify MRR at risk, and track weekly trends. |

## Data Privacy & Sanitization

To protect confidentiality, this portfolio does not include:

- Customer names
- Real account IDs
- Real emails or phone numbers
- Internal URLs
- Private payloads
- Proprietary screenshots
- Sensitive revenue details

Where needed, examples were rewritten using neutral terms such as:

- Customer A
- Account 001
- Location A / Location B
- CRM system
- Support platform
- Review platform
